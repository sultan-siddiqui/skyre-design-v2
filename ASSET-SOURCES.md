## Brand artwork and fonts

The logo, symbol and crescent pattern SVGs preserve the original artwork supplied in Sky Re's brand kit. The user-supplied `assets/sky-re-crescent-bg.png` remains in the asset archive. The current video backdrop uses the exact inline SVG pattern from the benchmark Core Philosophy section, including its sky/gold colours, 54 px repeat, 13% opacity and radial fade.

The footer now uses the user-supplied `assets/skyre-footer-logo.png` across all page views. CSS hides only its transparent outer padding. The original parent-version Home hero uses the supplied `assets/hero-icon-art.png` as a foreground overlay, with its two primary tips aligned to the right-edge and bottom-edge midpoints of the photograph. Both PNG files remain unchanged; the crescent uses uniform scaling and a small responsive orientation adjustment to fit those points.

Cairo is the approved first-pass font. Four weights are hosted locally. Keep `assets/OFL-Cairo.txt` and `assets/Cairo-font-notices.txt` with the fonts.
